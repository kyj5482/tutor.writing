# Writing Templates

Ten **daily** templates, a bonus ACE Write round, and four **growth** templates that carry
a student from a paragraph to university-level writing. Each template has **3 tiers** — the
tutor picks the tier from the student's profile. Higher tiers earn bonus XP (see
`game/rules.md`).

## Two kinds of template

**Daily (01–10)** — one sitting, one entry. These are the habit. They never get harder than
the student's current tier, and they're what most sessions use.

**Growth (12–15)** — these unlock the top of the 🪜 Writing Ladder. They are *longer*, but
they are **not meant to be written in one day.** Each one comes with a 3–4 day build plan:
one normal-sized chunk per day, each day its own journal entry with its own XP, streak
intact. The daily effort never changes — the pieces just add up into something big.

> This matters more than it sounds. A 350-word essay is intimidating; "write your thesis
> and one paragraph, like every other day" is not. Same essay, no extra load.

Run a growth template roughly **once a week**, or when a student finishes a book — never
two in a row, and never at the cost of the daily habit.

## Picking by today's reading amount

| Read today | Best fits |
|------------|-----------|
| Just a few pages | 09 Quick Write 3-2-1 · 07 Golden Line · 02 Prediction |
| About a chapter | 01 Summary · 03 Character Diary · 04 Opinion · 05 Letter · 08 Connection |
| A lot / a big event happened | 06 Rewrite the Scene · 04 Opinion · 01 Summary |
| Finished the book! | 10 Book Review |

## Growth templates — the ladder to university writing

| # | Template | Ladder stage | Build over | Unlocks |
|---|----------|--------------|-----------|---------|
| 12 | **Essay** | 🏛️ Essay Writer (4) | 3 days | thesis → evidence → an ending that adds |
| 13 | **Debate** | ⚖️ Debater (5) | 1–2 days | making the other side sound right, then answering it |
| 14 | **Craft Analysis** | 🔬 Critic (6) | 1–2 days | what a technique costs and what it buys |
| 15 | **Comparative Essay** | 🎓 Scholar (7) | 4 days | one argument held across two books |

Each maps to exactly one stage on the Writing Ladder, so a student who picks a goal on the
portfolio knows precisely which template gets them there.

**Prerequisites are soft, not gates.** A student who wants to try template 12 before
they've "finished" stage 3 should absolutely try it — at Tier 1, with the frame. Ambition
is not something to ration.
