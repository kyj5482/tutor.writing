# 16 — Reading Log

**Skill:** the daily record — what you read, what happened, what you think about it
**Best when:** school days. Any amount of reading fits — five pages or five chapters.
**Used by:** `/today` (the school-day session). Every log sits next to a 🔧 **Yesterday's fix**
— one sentence from yesterday's log, fixed by the student — so one short sitting does two jobs.

Three lines, always the same three. The sameness is the point: on a school night nobody has to
decide what to write, only what to *say*.

## Tier 1 (short and true)

- 📖 **Read:** book title + how much (pages or chapters)
- 🎬 **What happened:** 2 sentences, with the characters' names
- 💭 **My take:** 1 sentence with *because*

✅ Checklist: names in the "what happened," a *because* in the take, capitals and end marks.

## Tier 2 (a take you could argue with)

- 📖 **Read:** book title + how much
- 🎬 **What happened:** 3 sentences in order — one of them starts with a time word
  (*then · after · when · by the end*)
- 💭 **My take:** *"I think ___ because ___."* — then **one detail** from today's pages that
  proves it
- ✨ **Word or line** (optional): one word or short quote you noticed

✅ Checklist: time word, a claim someone could disagree with, a real detail as proof.

## Tier 3 (Shape A in miniature)

- 📖 **Read:** book title + how much
- 🎬 **What happened:** 2–3 sentences — **join two events in one sentence** with
  *while · after · although · when*
- 💭 **My take:** claim → a **short embedded quote** from today → why it matters (2–3 sentences)

✅ Checklist: one joined sentence, a quote inside your own sentence, a last line that **adds**
instead of repeating.

## What the log is not

- **Not a summary contest.** Two true sentences beat eight vague ones.
- **Not where length grows.** Paragraphs and shapes are `/weekend` work; the log grows by one
  better sentence at a time.
- **Not corrected tonight.** Tomorrow's 🔧 fix card comes *from* this log — the student fixes it
  themselves, with the rule, before they write the next one.
