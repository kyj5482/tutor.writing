# Mini-lesson — One idea, one paragraph

**For:** Jia. **Status: ✅ TAUGHT 2026-07-30.** **Do not re-explain the rule.**
**Time:** 3 minutes. **Reward:** +5 XP.

---

## ✅ How it went (2026-07-30)

Ran exactly as written below, on her **Book Review for *Half-Blood Prince*** — the same template
as the 07-20 review used in Step 1, which made the before/after land harder than planned.

**What worked, and what to reuse:**

1. **Step 1 as written is the whole lesson.** Her own 07-20 review, twice, two blank lines added,
   not one word changed. She never answered "which one looks like a real book review?" in words —
   she just went and did the task. ➡️ **With her, showing beats asking-then-showing.** Don't wait
   for the verbal answer; it isn't coming and it isn't needed.
2. **The OFF switch first-time, per the 07-27 finding.** Taught with her own sentence
   *"Dumbledore and Harry work together."* → still job #1, clean pass, nothing to change. She did
   not over-apply the rule once, unlike compound subjects on 07-27. **The OFF switch works.**
3. **Step 4's practice item was tonight's own entry, not 07-24** — better. Handed back as 7
   numbered sentences: "find the two spots where the job changes, just say the numbers."
4. ⭐ **The line that actually unlocked it:** her first guess was *"after 5"* — correct, and the
   easier break (verdict → rating). She could not see the big one until asked:
   > ### "Where did you stop **telling** and start **judging**?"
   Answer came back instantly: **"after 3."** ➡️ **That question is the trick to keep for her** —
   more than "new job, new paragraph," which is the rule but not the finder. Reuse the exact words.

**Result:** 🧱 Paragraph Pro — **the first paragraph break in 20 entries** — and the ceiling on
her Structure tier is gone. Total elapsed: under three minutes, two messages.

**Follow-up, as Step 6 says:** it is **not** on the Watch list. Make *"break this into
paragraphs"* the 🚀 stretch on the next few long entries; if one comes back as a block, say five
words — ***"where did the job change?"*** — and let her find it.

## Why it was queued (kept for the record)

Jia has never used a paragraph break. Not once in 18 entries — every long piece she has
written is a single block:

- **06-30** — a 9-sentence rewrite, one block
- **07-20** — her Book Review: hook, summary, strength, weakness, rating, recommendation.
  **Six different jobs, zero breaks.**
- **07-24** — three events, two word-analyses, and a question, all in one paragraph

But she is already carrying a verb Watch item, and the rule is **one improvement at a time**.
Stacking a second structural fix on top would turn a good week into a correction list.
Wait for the clear. It's coming.

**Why it's worth waiting for:** this is the highest-value-per-minute fix in her whole
profile. Her *content* is already Tier 2 — the ideas are organized, the transitions are
there. Only the *look* of it is Tier 1. Three Enter keys would make her 07-20 review read
like a real review, with no new writing at all. That's a rare kind of win and she should
get to feel it.

## Step 1 — Show her, don't tell her

Take her own 07-20 Book Review and put it on screen twice — once as she wrote it, once with
breaks added and **not one word changed**:

> Have you ever wondered what will happen if no one believes you? *Harry Potter and the
> Order of the Phoenix* by JK Rowling is about Harry facing off Voldemort again…
>
> The best thing about this book is that no one believed Harry and Dumbledore that
> Voldemort was back…
>
> One thing that could be better is if Professor Umbridge died in the end. I give it five
> out of five stars because even though it is really long, every chapter made me want to
> keep reading.

Then ask: **"Which one looks like a real book review?"**

Let her answer. The point lands harder when she says it. Then tell her the good part:
**every word was already hers.** She didn't need to write better — her writing was already
there, it just needed air.

## Step 2 — The rule (one sentence)

> ### When you switch to a new job, press Enter twice.

"Job" is the useful word here, and her Book Review already has them clearly separated:

| Paragraph | Its job |
|-----------|---------|
| 1 | hook + what the book is about |
| 2 | what was good |
| 3 | what could be better + rating + who should read it |

She *already* wrote in these three jobs. She just never pressed Enter between them.

## Step 3 — The test

> **Read your piece and ask at each sentence: "Am I still doing the same job?"**
> Still summarizing? Same paragraph. Started giving your opinion? **New paragraph.**

Rough guide for her grade: if a paragraph runs past **5–6 sentences**, there are probably
two jobs hiding in it.

## Step 4 — Practice on something she already wrote

Best practice item is her **07-24 Quick Write** — it has three obvious jobs (the 3 events,
the 2 words, the 1 question) crammed into one block. Ask her to split it. No new writing,
just Enter keys. Takes 30 seconds and she'll see the effect instantly.

## Step 5 — The trick she keeps

### ⏎ New job, new paragraph

> Before you save: find the sentence where you **stopped telling and started judging**
> (or stopped one topic and started another). Put a blank line there.

Usually there are only one or two such spots. That's the whole skill.

## Step 6 — After the lesson

- Award **+5 XP**, log as `lesson: paragraph breaks`.
- **Don't add it to the Watch list.** Instead, make it the 🚀 stretch challenge for the
  next few entries: *"Break this into 2 paragraphs"* — concrete and instantly doable,
  which is the stretch format she reliably completes.
- Once it's automatic, her Structure skill is knocking on Tier 3.

지아는 지금까지 **단 한 번도 문단을 나눈 적이 없습니다** — 긴 글은 모두 한 덩어리예요. 특히
07-20 독후감은 훅·줄거리·장점·단점·별점·추천까지 **여섯 가지 역할**이 한 문단에 들어 있습니다.

좋은 소식은, 이건 **새로 쓸 것이 하나도 없는 수정**이라는 점입니다. 내용과 순서는 이미 잘 짜여
있어서, 엔터 두 번이면 글이 훨씬 완성도 높아 보입니다. 시간 대비 효과가 가장 큰 항목이에요.

다만 지금은 **동사 항목이 정리된 뒤로 미뤄뒀습니다.** 한 번에 한 가지만 고치는 것이 원칙이라,
두 가지를 동시에 지적하면 칭찬받아야 할 주가 지적 목록이 되어버리기 때문입니다.
