# Mini-lesson — "a" vs "an" (it's about **sound**, not spelling)

**For:** both. **Time:** 90 seconds — this is the quickest win in the folder.
**Reward:** +5 XP.
**Run it when:** there's a spare minute at the end of a session. It has never once been
addressed in 35 entries, and it has appeared **5 times**.

---

## Why this one is worth 90 seconds

Neither student has ever been corrected on this — not once. It's small, it's easy, and it
shows up in exactly the sentences they're proudest of:

**Jaei:**
- *"This book did **a excellent** job in the pace and the twists"* (07-11)
- *"The book does **a incredible** job in showing the event in 1st person"* (07-23)

**Jia:**
- *"Hermione is taking **a O.W.L.S.** test"* (07-15)
- *"Snape did **a unbreakable** vow"* — twice in one entry (07-24)

Both of Jaei's are in his **hook or craft sentence** — the line a reader notices most.
Fixing this makes their best sentences land cleanly, which is a good thing to tell them.

## The rule (one sentence)

> ### Use **an** when the next word *starts with a vowel sound*: a, e, i, o, u.

- an **e**xcellent job · an **i**ncredible job · an **u**nbreakable vow · an **a**pple

## The part that makes it click — it's the SOUND, not the letter

This is the whole lesson, and it's the bit nobody tells kids:

| Looks like it should be… | but you say | because it **sounds** like |
|---|---|---|
| **a** hour | **an** hour | "**ow**-er" — vowel sound (the h is silent) |
| **an** university | **a** university | "**yoo**-niversity" — starts with a *y* sound |
| **an** one-way street | **a** one-way street | "**wun**-way" — starts with a *w* sound |

So the test isn't *"does it start with a vowel letter?"* It's **"open your mouth and say it."**

**And Jia's O.W.L.S. is the perfect example** — say the letter **O** out loud: *"oh"*. Vowel
sound. So it's **an O.W.L.S. test**, even though O.W.L.S. is an abbreviation. Use her own
sentence for this; it's more convincing than any invented example.

## Practice out loud (3 items, 20 seconds)

1. ____ unbreakable vow
2. ____ hour later
3. ____ 39 Clues book

*(an · an · a — "thirty-nine" starts with a **th** sound)*

## The trick they keep

### 👄 Say the next word first

> Before you write *a* or *an*, say the **next word** out loud.
> Mouth opens on a vowel sound → **an**.

That's it. This one is genuinely finished in 90 seconds — no follow-up needed, no Watch-list
row. Just correct it silently in feedback for a couple of weeks if it reappears.

**Don't put this on the Watch list.** The Watch list is capped at 2 items for a reason, and
both students already have theirs filled with something harder. This is a footnote fix, and
treating a footnote like a campaign is how a kind system turns into a nagging one.

## 부모님께

두 아이 모두 **a / an** 실수가 다섯 번 있었는데, 지금까지 한 번도 지적된 적이 없습니다.
90초면 끝나는 아주 쉬운 항목이에요.

핵심은 **철자가 아니라 소리**라는 점입니다 — "an hour"(h는 묵음이라 모음 소리), "a university"
("유"로 시작). 그래서 *다음 단어를 소리 내어 말해보고* 입이 모음으로 열리면 **an**을 쓰면 됩니다.

이 항목은 일부러 Watch list에 넣지 않았습니다. Watch list는 최대 2개까지만 유지하는 것이 원칙이고
(잔소리가 되지 않도록), 두 아이 모두 이미 더 중요한 항목이 들어가 있기 때문입니다.
