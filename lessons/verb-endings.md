# Mini-lesson — Verb endings you can hear (-ed / -s)

**For:** Jia. **Time:** 3 minutes. **Reward:** +5 XP.
**Run it when:** the endings half of her verb Watch item stalls again after the
compound-subject lesson. Don't run both lessons in the same session.

---

## Step 1 — Start with the win, not the problem

> "You just cleared your whole spelling Watch item — 3 out of 3 — using one habit: reading
> out loud before you save. Today we point that exact same habit at verbs. You already
> own the tool."

This is true and it matters. Her spelling item went 0/3 → cleared in five days once the
read-aloud habit took hold (07-20, 07-23, 07-24). She has already proved the method works.

## Step 2 — Show her the pattern in her own writing

These are all hers, from her first entry to her most recent:

| Date | What she wrote | What her ear catches |
|------|----------------|----------------------|
| 06-09 | *"Their grandmother **bring** a lot of presents"* | brought |
| 07-01 | *"did not **knew** how"* | know |
| 07-14 | *"This **show** that Snape…"* | show**s** |
| 07-16 | *"Voldemort was the one who **fighted** Harry"* | fought |
| 07-20 | *"Have you ever **wonder**"* | wonder**ed** |
| 07-20 | *"Sirius Black **die**"* | die**d** |
| 07-23 | *"what he **stoled**"* | stole |

Then say the honest thing:

> "Every single one of these, you fixed the second you read it out loud. On 07-23 you caught
> *'ask' → 'asked'* and *'show' → 'shows'* by yourself. **Your ear is not the problem.
> The timing is** — the reading-aloud is happening after you save instead of before."

## Step 3 — The rule (two sentences)

> ### If it already happened, the verb ends in the past. If one person is doing it right now, the verb takes **-s**.

And the one that trips her up most:

> ### Some verbs are already past — they don't take -ed on top.
> **stole** (not stoled) · **fought** (not fighted) · **knew** · **brought** · **told** · **ran**

Point out the pattern to her: *stoled* and *fighted* are actually a **smart** mistake. She
knew the sentence needed past tense and applied the -ed rule correctly — the verb just
happens to be an irregular one that already did the job. That's a rule-follower's error,
not a careless one. Say so.

## Step 4 — The "one or many, now or before" test

Two questions, in this order:

1. **Now or before?** → before = past form (*died*, *stole*, *fought*)
2. **One person or more?** → one = add **-s** (*This show**s*** · *no one believe**s***)

Practice out loud, 3 items:

1. Yesterday Harry ____ (find / found) the diary.
2. This ____ (show / shows) that Snape is lying.
3. Last year Voldemort ____ (stoled / stole) the prophecy.

*(found · shows · stole)*

## Step 5 — The trick she keeps

### 🔊 The Verb-Only Read

> Before you save, read your writing out loud — but **only the verbs**, slowly.
> *"wondered… is… believed… died…"*
> When one sounds wrong, it **is** wrong.

Reading the whole piece aloud lets her brain smooth over the small words. Reading **only the
verbs** strips them out where nothing can hide. It takes about ten seconds.

**Important — tell her the limit of this trick:** it will *not* catch *"Snape and Malfoy is"*,
because that one sounds fine. That one needs the ✌️ two-finger check from
`compound-subjects.md`. Two different problems, two different tools — knowing which is which
is half the skill.

## Step 6 — After the lesson

- Award **+5 XP**, log as `lesson: verb endings`.
- Next 3 sessions: if an ending slips, say **"verb-only read"** — don't re-teach.
- Her ear is fast and reliable. Expect this one to clear quickly once the check moves to
  *before* saving. When it clears, say out loud that she has now cleared **two** Watch items
  with the same habit — that's a story about who she is, not just a fixed error.

## 부모님께

지아의 동사 어미 실수(-ed/-s)는 첫 글(06-09)부터 최근 글(07-24)까지 계속 나타납니다. 하지만
**소리 내어 읽으면 지아는 거의 100% 스스로 잡아냅니다** — 실제로 07-23에는 두 개를 혼자
고쳤어요. 즉 실력 문제가 아니라 **점검 시점**의 문제입니다(저장한 *뒤에* 읽고 있어요).

특히 "stoled", "fighted" 같은 실수는 오히려 **규칙을 잘 지킨 결과**입니다 — 과거형이 필요한 걸
알고 -ed를 붙였는데, 그 동사가 불규칙 동사였을 뿐이에요. 혼내실 일이 전혀 아닙니다.

새로 알려준 방법은 **"동사만 소리 내어 읽기"**입니다. 글 전체를 읽으면 뇌가 부드럽게 넘어가버려서,
동사만 천천히 읽는 게 훨씬 잘 잡힙니다. 10초면 됩니다.
