# Tutor card — Jia

*How to teach **this** kid. Read with `profile.md` at the start of every session.
**This is a card, not a diary** — keep it under ~80 lines. Session-by-session history lives in
`archive/tutor-full-2026-08-18.md`; add a finding here only when it changes what you'd *do*.*

## Who she is

- **Immersive reader with real stamina.** Harry Potter 3→7 (~3,400 pages) in 8 weeks. Her limit is
  time, not attention.
- **What hooks her:** friendship and loyalty, unfairness done to someone she likes, secrets.
- **She is a noticer.** *"Sirius Black was first really happy for him but became moodier and
  surlier."* (07-05) — nobody asked.
- **Her subject is what a choice COSTS someone.** Sirius going quiet · Slughorn's guilt · Tris
  sparing Tobias. Eight entries, never prompted.
- **She is honest and a little funny** — *"...if Professor Umbridge died in the end."* Never sand
  this off.
- ⭐ **She sources her own books** — five self-found since 08-17. A "what next?" menu is a courtesy,
  not a rescue: ask what she wants *after a book like this one*, and stop there.
- ⭐ **She revises her CHOICES, not just her sentences** (08-17, swapped her own golden line unasked).
  Name it: *"that's a writer being unsatisfied."*

## ⚠️ Her ear is excellent. Her eye is not.

She catches her own errors every time she reads aloud (*ask→asked*, *Harry like→Harry likes*,
*a very long time* 09-02) — seven sessions running. Silent proofreading catches her nothing.
**But match the tool to the error:**

| Error type | Tool | Why |
|-----------|------|-----|
| Endings (-ed / -s), tense, word choice | 🔊 **read aloud** | She hears it instantly |
| Agreement with *and* (*"Neville and Luna says"*) | 📖 **the rule, then the mic** | Sounds fine, so the ear can't *find* it — but once she knows the rule, the read-aloud is where she *applies* it (08-18) |
| Quotation marks, possessive `'s` | ✌️ **a count** | Marks are silent; you can't hear punctuation |

⭐ **The mic cannot fix silent marks — but the RULE can.** 08-23 she read aloud and the missing
quotation marks stayed missing. Told the rule once, she typed both quotes correctly on 08-30.
**Name the mark, then have her TYPE it. Never re-read it.**

🔴 **Never ask for a keystroke in the same message as the mic (08-30).** Dictation cannot type a
break. Get the keystroke in typed text FIRST, then offer the mic.

## How to run a session with her

- ⏱️ **Two rounds, then save. The most important line on the card.** 08-17: four rounds bought 58
  words, and every decline came after the third ask. **Fewer, bigger questions.**
- 🔢 **One ask per message — she completes the SMALLEST ones (08-30).** Given five, she did the two
  easiest and dropped three. **Menus of tasks get skipped.** Ask for the hardest thing alone.
- **Ask both halves in ONE question.** Split in two, she answers the first and stops (08-17: gave
  the word, never the *because*). Say *"Which word — and who normally says it?"*
- **Warm-up works on her** (unlike Jaei). Greeting, badges, level — keep the game visible.
- **Concrete gets done; abstract gets declined.** Every stretch = a fill-in-the-blank.
- **Show both slots on screen.** *"This shows ______. It also means ______."* fixed in one night
  what three sessions of "elaborate" never moved.
- **Menus of choices get picked; menus of tasks get skipped.**
- **Choosing works; inventing does not.** Asked to invent a sentence the author *could* have
  written, she returned the boring situation. Convert invention into choice.
- 💰 **Unclaimed XP as a table is the strongest device on this card.** A **+10 unclaimed** row got
  her to *ask* for the breaks (08-30) and to type one (09-02). **When a habit stalls, put a number
  on it.**
- **Never soften a rule.** She audits the scoreboard and takes a straight *"no, here's the line"* well.
- **After any gap: name the permanent things first, the reset once, and never ask where she was.**
- **Her templates decide what is measurable.** To score a habit, the frame must demand it.
- **Never score a mishearing as her error.** 09-02 the mic turned *"In the book, Tris"* into *"In the
  workplace, guests"* — nearly every noun wrong. **Read-aloud is for her EAR, not for her text:**
  award the +5, keep her typed version, and take only the fixes she names (09-02 she added the
  missing *a* in *"a very long time"*).

## Where she is now

**Owns:** structure & transitions · explanation with *because* · specific names · honest opinions ·
ACE format · a quote inside a main entry (07-27) · paragraph breaks when asked (07-30) · the
two-sentence explanation (08-03) · conceding a counterargument (08-05).

**Next:** 📌 **three breaks, typed by her.** The keystroke barrier fell 09-02 — she now owns
Shift+Enter. Shape C (3 ¶) is the last thing between her and 🏛️ Essay Writer.

**Untaught, not yet raised:** *a / an* before a vowel · dropped period-space between sentences ·
*stimulation* for *simulation* (08-30, ×2 — told once, not scored).

🎉 **SOLVED 09-02 — and the lesson is bigger than the break.** For six sessions she was told "press
Enter twice." **In Claude Code, Enter *sends* the message** — the ask was physically impossible, and
she failed it five times looking like the problem. Told **Shift+Enter**, she did it on the next try.
➡️ **When a kid fails the same simple ask three times, stop coaching and check whether the ask is
possible.** Still point at her actual words: *"Shift+Enter twice, right before ___."*

## Talk to her like this

✅ *"Read that sentence out loud — you'll hear it."* (name her superpower)
✅ *"You noticed which NAME he uses. That's what real readers do."*
✅ *"Which one did the author reject?"* (choosing works; inventing does not)
✅ *"That's a writer being unsatisfied."* (when she throws out her own choice)
✅ *"**Shift+Enter twice**, right before the word **But**."* (plain Enter *sends* — never say it)

❌ *"Check your verbs."* → say **read aloud** instead
❌ *"Add a few sentences about why it mattered."* → too open, she'll decline
❌ *"Write more."* → ask for a **move**, never a word count
❌ **Four rounds of questions for one paragraph** (see 08-17)
❌ Correcting her honesty or her jokes into something more polite

## Korean note for the parent (지아)

지아는 **관찰자형 작가**입니다 — 아무도 묻지 않은 걸 스스로 포착하고, 특히 **"어떤 선택이 그 사람에게
무엇을 치르게 하는가"**를 계속 씁니다.

**지아는 '눈'이 아니라 '귀'로 글을 고칩니다.** 소리 내어 읽으면 자기 실수를 거의 다 잡아냅니다. 다만
*"두 사람인데 is를 쓰는"* 실수와 `'s`는 소리로 안 잡히니 **규칙**과 **세어보기**로 따로 잡습니다.

**8월 17~19일에 배운 것:** 질문을 네 번 하면 세 번째 이후로 전부 거절합니다. 그래서 **질문 두 번 안에**
끝냅니다. 그리고 요즘 지아는 **책을 스스로 골라 하루에 한 권씩** 읽습니다 — 책을 골라줄 필요가 없어요.

남은 과제는 **길이**입니다. "더 써라" 대신 *동작 하나*를 요구합니다. 참고로 9월 2일에 알게 된 것:
여섯 번이나 안 되던 문단 나누기는 지아 잘못이 아니라 **Enter를 누르면 메시지가 전송되는** 프로그램
구조 때문이었습니다. **Shift+Enter**라고 한 번 알려주니 바로 해냈습니다.
