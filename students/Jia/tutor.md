## Who she is

- **Immersive, one-world-deep reader with real stamina.** Harry Potter books 3→7 (~3,400 pages)
  between 06-09 and 08-06, including the 766-page *Order of the Phoenix*. Her limit is time, not
  attention.
- **But she can choose for herself, and she chose *away* from her comfort.** During an 11-day gap
  she found and finished **The Honest Truth** — realistic fiction, no magic — unaided (08-17).
  "Commits and stays" is still true; **"needs to be handed the next book" is not.** At the next
  between-books moment, ask what she wants **after a book like this one**.
- **What hooks her:** friendship and loyalty, unfairness done to someone she likes, secrets
  and backstory.
- **She is a noticer.** *"Sirius Black was first really happy for him but became moodier and
  surlier… and talk less to everyone."* (07-05) Nobody asked her about Sirius's mood.
- **Her subject is what a choice COSTS someone.** Sirius going quiet · Slughorn's guilt · Lupin
  afraid of what he passed to his child · McGonagall trading her life. Six entries, one instinct,
  never prompted.
- **She is honest and a little funny** — *"One thing that could be better is if Professor Umbridge
  died in the end."* Never sand this off.
- ⭐ **New (08-17): she revises her CHOICES now, not just her sentences.** She swapped her own
  golden line mid-session for a harder one, unasked. **Name it when it happens** — *"that's a
  writer being unsatisfied."* Watch for a third instance.

## ⚠️ Her ear is excellent. Her eye is not.

Every time she reads aloud she catches her own errors (*ask→asked*, *show→shows*,
*Harry like→Harry likes*) — six consecutive sessions where her ear beat her eye. Silent
proofreading catches her nothing. **But match the tool to the error:**

| Error type | Tool | Why |
|-----------|------|-----|
| Endings (-ed / -s), tense, word choice | 🔊 **read aloud** | She hears it instantly |
| Agreement with *and* (*"Neville and Luna says"*) | 📖 **the rule** | It *sounds* fine — the ear will never catch it |
| Quotation marks, possessive `'s` | ✌️ **a count** | Marks are silent; you can't hear punctuation |

## How to run a session with her

- ⏱️ **Two rounds, then save. This is the most important line on the card.** On 08-17 four rounds
  of scaffolding bought 58 words, and **every decline came after the third ask** — the 🚀, the 🎤
  and the ACE all landed after she had already said *"I'm good."* The mic had been her habit for
  nine straight sessions; it broke on the fourth ask, not because the habit is weak.
  **Fewer, bigger questions — and put the mic offer EARLY**, before the writing is finished.
- **Ask both halves in ONE question.** Split into two prompts, she answers the first and stops:
  she gave *"the word I keep looking at is die"* and never gave the *because* — which would have
  been her Style promotion. Say *"Which word — and who normally says it?"*
- **Warm-up works on her** (unlike Jaei). Greeting, badges, level — keep the game visible.
- **Concrete gets done; abstract gets declined.** Write every stretch as a fill-in-the-blank with
  an example sentence.
- **Show both slots on screen.** *"This shows ______. It also means ______."* fixed in one night
  what three sessions of asking her to elaborate never moved.
- **Choosing works; inventing does not.** Every strength she has is anchored to text that *is* in
  the book. Asked to invent a sentence the author *could* have written, she returned the boring
  situation instead. Convert invention into choice: *"Which one did the author reject?"*
- **Menus of choices get picked; menus of tasks get skipped.** She chose 🎓 Scholar — the hardest
  stage — from a pick-one menu with sample sentences.
- 💰 **Unclaimed XP, shown as a concrete table, is the strongest forward-booking device found.**
  She committed to the next session herself on a night she had declined three things. It works
  because it's attached to work she **hasn't done yet**, with a number on it.
- **Never soften a rule to avoid disappointing her.** She audits the scoreboard (08-02, 08-17) and
  takes a straight *"no, here's the line"* well. Softening it would break what makes XP mean
  anything to her.
- **After any gap: name the permanent things first, the reset once, and never ask where she was.**
- **Her templates decide what is even measurable.** Six straight pieces had no quotation marks
  because nothing in the frame required one. If you want to score a habit, the frame must demand it.
- **Never score a mishearing as her error.** Budget one confirm message per read-aloud.

## Korean note for the parent (지아)

지아는 **관찰자형 작가**입니다. 아무도 묻지 않은 작은 것을 스스로 포착해요 — 특히 **"어떤 선택이
그 사람에게 무엇을 치르게 하는가"**를 계속 씁니다. 여섯 편 연속으로, 시킨 적 없이요.

가장 중요한 발견: **지아는 '눈'이 아니라 '귀'로 글을 고칩니다.** 소리 내어 읽으면 자기 실수를 거의
다 잡아냅니다. 다만 *"두 사람인데 is를 쓰는"* 실수와 따옴표·`'s` 같은 건 소리로 안 잡히니 **규칙**과
**세어보기**로 따로 잡습니다.

**8월 17일에 중요한 걸 배웠습니다.** 한 문단 쓰는 데 질문을 네 번 했더니, 세 번째 질문 이후로 지아가
전부 거절했어요(마이크 읽기 포함 — 아홉 세션 연속 하던 습관인데도요). 아이가 지친 게 아니라 **과정이
길었던 겁니다.** 그래서 앞으로는 **질문 두 번 안에 끝냅니다.**

지금 유일한 과제는 **길이**입니다. 두 달 동안 60~100단어, 거의 전부 한 문단이었어요. "더 써라" 대신
**"두 번째 문단은 But으로 시작해"** 처럼 *동작 하나*를 더 요구합니다. 길이는 따라옵니다.
