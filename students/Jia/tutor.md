# Tutor card — Jia

*How to teach **this** kid. Read with `profile.md` every session. **A card, not a diary** — history
lives in `archive/tutor-full-2026-08-18.md`; add a finding only when it changes what you'd *do*.*

---

## Who she is

- **Immersive reader with real stamina.** HP 3→7 (~3,400 pages) in 8 weeks. Limit is time, not
  attention.
- **What hooks her:** friendship and loyalty, unfairness done to someone she likes, secrets.
- **She is a noticer.** *"Sirius Black... became moodier and surlier."* (07-05) — nobody asked.
- **Her subject is what a choice COSTS someone.** Sirius going quiet · Slughorn's guilt · Tris
  sparing Tobias. Eight entries, never prompted.
- **She is honest and a little funny** — *"...if Professor Umbridge died in the end."* Never sand
  this off.
- ⭐ **She sources her own books** — five self-found since 08-17. A "what next?" menu is a courtesy,
  not a rescue: ask what she wants *after a book like this one*, and stop there.
- ⭐ **She revises her CHOICES, not just her sentences** (08-17). Name it: *"that's a writer being
  unsatisfied."*

## ⚠️ Her ear is excellent. Her eye is not.

She catches her own errors every time she reads aloud — seven sessions running, and 09-03 she
supplied **both** missing words in *"going into the chocolate factory"* when told only that a word
was missing. Silent proofreading catches her nothing. **But match the tool to the error:**

| Error type | Tool | Why |
|-----------|------|-----|
| Endings (-ed / -s), tense, word choice | 🔊 **read aloud** | She hears it instantly |
| Agreement with *and* (*"Neville and Luna says"*) | 📖 **the rule, then the mic** | Sounds fine, so the ear can't *find* it; once she knows the rule, the mic is where she *applies* it (08-18) |
| Quotation marks, possessive `'s` | ✌️ **a count** | Marks are silent; you can't hear punctuation |

⭐ **The mic cannot fix silent marks — the RULE can.** 08-23 reading aloud left the missing quotes
missing; told the rule once, she typed both correctly on 08-30. **Name the mark, then have her TYPE
it. Never re-read it.**

🔴 **Never ask for a keystroke in the same message as the mic (08-30).** Dictation cannot type a
break. Get the keystroke in typed text FIRST, then offer the mic.

## How to run a session with her

- ⏱️ **Two rounds, then save. The most important line on the card.** 08-17: four rounds bought 58
  words, and every decline came after the third ask. **Fewer, bigger questions.**
- 🔢 **One ask per message — she completes the SMALLEST ones (08-30).** Given five, she did the two
  easiest and dropped three. **Menus of tasks get skipped.** Ask for the hardest thing alone.
- **Ask both halves in ONE question.** Split in two, she answers the first and stops (08-17: gave
  the word, never the *because*). Say *"Which word — and who normally says it?"*
- **Warm-up works on her** (unlike Jaei). Greeting, badges, level — keep the game visible.
- **Concrete gets done; abstract gets declined.** Every stretch = fill-in-the-blank.
- **Show both slots on screen.** *"This shows ______. It also means ______."* fixed in one night
  what three sessions of "elaborate" never moved.
- **She stops cleanly when she's done** (09-03: *"I'm done for the day now"*). Take it at face
  value and close warm — never re-offer the thing she just passed on.
- **Menus of choices get picked; menus of tasks get skipped.**
- **Choosing works; inventing does not.** Convert invention into choice.
- 💰 **Unclaimed XP as a table is the strongest device on this card.** A **+10 unclaimed** row got
  her to *ask* for the breaks (08-30) and to type one (09-02). **When a habit stalls, put a number
  on it.**
- **Never soften a rule.** She audits the scoreboard and takes a straight *"no, here's the line"* well.
- **After a gap: permanent things first, the reset once, never ask where she was.**
- 🔴 **Blanks are optional; questions are not (09-03).** A frame with a `'s` blank and an
  *"X and Y ___"* blank produced neither — she fills the blanks she has content for and routes
  around the rest. **To score a habit, put it in the QUESTION, not in a slot.**
- 🔴 **The mic always beats the ACE round when both are offered** (09-02, 09-03). If the ACE round
  is what matters that day, offer it **alone** — no mic, no choices, no second item.
- **Never score a mishearing as her error.** The mic mangles nearly every proper noun (09-02 *Tris*
  → *"guests"*; 09-03 *Roald Dahl* → *"Royal Dial"*). **Read-aloud is for her EAR, not her text:**
  award the +5, keep her typed version, take only the fixes she names.

ACE format · a quote in a main entry (07-27) · the two-sentence explanation (08-03) · conceding a
counterargument (08-05) · **the ⇧+Enter break (09-02, 09-03)**.

**Next:** 📌 **her first Tier-3 entry.** 09-04 audit: **36 entries, zero at Tier 3** — not because
she can't (she does every Tier-3 move separately) but because nobody has offered it. ➡️ **Offer the
harder tier by default now and let her drop, the way Jaei gets it.** Then Shape C (3 ¶).

**Untaught, not yet raised:** *a / an* before a vowel · dropped period-space · noun-for-adjective
(*"very adventures book"*, 09-03).

🎉 **SOLVED 09-02, held 09-03.** For six sessions she was told "press Enter twice" — but **in Claude
Code, Enter *sends*.** The ask was impossible, and she failed it five times looking like the problem.
Told **Shift+Enter**, she did it next try, and again the next session. ➡️ **When a kid fails the same
simple ask three times, check whether the ask is possible.** Point at her words: *"⇧+Enter, right
before ___."*

## Talk to her like this

✅ *"Read that sentence out loud — you'll hear it."* (name her superpower)
✅ *"Which one did the author reject?"* (choosing works; inventing does not)
✅ *"That's a writer being unsatisfied."* (when she throws out her own choice)
✅ *"**Shift+Enter twice**, right before the word **But**."* (plain Enter *sends* — never say it)

❌ *"Check your verbs."* → say **read aloud** instead
❌ *"Add a few sentences about why it mattered."* → too open, she'll decline
❌ *"Write more."* → ask for a **move**, never a word count
❌ **Four rounds of questions for one paragraph** (see 08-17)
❌ Correcting her honesty or her jokes into something more polite

## Korean note for the parent (지아)

지아는 **관찰자형 작가**입니다 — 아무도 묻지 않은 걸 스스로 포착하고, 특히 **"어떤 선택이 그 사람에게
무엇을 치르게 하는가"**를 계속 씁니다.

**지아는 '눈'이 아니라 '귀'로 글을 고칩니다.** 소리 내어 읽으면 자기 실수를 거의 다 잡아냅니다. 다만
`'s`처럼 소리로 안 들리는 건 **규칙**과 **세어보기**로 따로 잡습니다. 질문은 **두 번 안에** 끝냅니다 —
세 번째부터는 거절합니다. 책은 **스스로 골라** 읽으니 골라줄 필요가 없습니다.

남은 과제는 **길이**입니다. "더 써라" 대신 *동작 하나*를 요구합니다. 참고로 9월 2일에 알게 된 것:
여섯 번이나 안 되던 문단 나누기는 지아 잘못이 아니라 **Enter를 누르면 메시지가 전송되는** 프로그램
구조 때문이었습니다. **Shift+Enter**라고 한 번 알려주니 바로 해냈습니다.
