## Tutor feedback

🌟 **Two things you did well**

1. **Four transitions, in order, on a several-chapter day.** *First… After that… Then… Finally…* — the Tier-2 checklist asks for three and she used four, across a stretch of book with a break-in, a Horcrux, and a kidnapping in it. This is the skill that got Structure promoted to Tier 2 back on 07-06 and it is now completely automatic; she doesn't think about it any more.
2. **The last two sentences do the hardest job in a summary: they leave the reader somewhere.** *"Now Harry knows that the Death Eaters got Luna so that Luna's father will trade Harry for Luna. Harry has to watch out for everyone because now everyone is in danger and might put Harry in danger."* That's not "and that's what I read" — it's the **trap** closing, and she saw its shape: the danger to Harry now runs *through the people he cares about*. Same instinct as *"save his life while he is trying to save other people's life"* (08-02). **Sixth entry running that she goes to what a situation costs somebody.**

🔧 **One thing to level up — and this one is a genuine discovery, not a correction.**

> *"where Harry, Ron, and Hermione **is** trying to find the Horcrux"* → ***are***

Here is why this matters more than an ordinary slip: **she ran her ✌️ two-finger check on that exact sentence, out loud, and it did not fire.** She has applied the compound-subject rule correctly and unprompted for four straight sessions (07-28, 07-29, 07-30, 07-31). So the rule is not gone.

What's gone is its **range**. The rule she was taught on 07-27 was *"**and** between **two** doers → plural verb"* — and she learned it exactly as stated. Tonight there were **three** names in a list, and the check she was holding didn't recognise the shape.

➡️ **The fix is a sharpening, not a re-teach:** ✌️ becomes *"count the doers — **any** number more than one takes the plural verb."* Two, three, ten. Harry **and** Ron **and** Hermione **are**.
