🔧 **One thing to level up: your side has details. The book's side doesn't.**

Count them:

🚀 **Stretch challenge (+10 XP, optional): one new sentence — find the *feeling* match.**

Not a repair, a new sentence. Her connection right now matches on the **event** (both rushing). The example matched on the **feeling**, and said so in one line:

> *"the worst part wasn't the swimming, it was everyone being nice about it"*

So:

Fill it in for the school-to-swimming rush. Whatever goes in that second blank is probably also true for Harry — and that's when a connection stops being "we both did X" and starts being real.
