## Tutor feedback

🌟 **Two things you did well:**

1. **You absorbed the objection to your own thesis instead of dodging it.** Your thesis says the series is about the ending, not the battle — and any reader can fire back *"the battle is ten books long."* Your answer: *"This was designed so that people would betray each other until the end."* The betrayal isn't evidence against you, it's the setup. That's a concession folded into the argument, and you did it without being asked.
2. **The paragraph escalates, which is what a second body paragraph is for.** Paragraph 1 said the ending mattered. This one says the ending was **engineered** — Grace built a hunt designed to make the branches tear each other apart, and the unity at the end was the plan the whole time. That's a bigger claim than paragraph 1, so it earns the last slot.

✅ **Watch list — clean, first draft.** *"all the branches united"* — plural subject, plural verb, no flag from me. **Clean streak 2/3.**

🔧 **One thing to level up (the paragraph promises proof and never delivers it):** Your last sentence is *"This is the proof of how the trust was built."* But nothing above it is proof — it's all assertion. You say *"that is what Grace planned"* and then *"The ending was just as what Grace planned"*: same claim twice, no evidence between them. And the quotation marks around *"Grace planned"* are around **your** words, not Haddix's — quotation marks are a promise that the book said this, exactly.

You already own the fix and you already found it. Saturday you hunted the page and came back with:
