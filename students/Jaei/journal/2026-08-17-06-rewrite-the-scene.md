## Tutor feedback

🌟 **You spent one of Messenger's own details as a writer's move — second time you've made
that withdrawal.**
*"Tugging out her eyelashes for third time since the conversation started."* Eyelash-tugging is
**Sophie's** tell, not a generic nervous habit — it's the thing Messenger reaches for when Sophie
is holding something in. You didn't invent a gesture; you used hers. Same move as Forkle's
*"You kids"* on 08-03, which you'd noticed on 07-16 and spent eighteen days later. Nobody
prompted either one.

🌟 **Her hands and her voice disagree, and that's the best sentence in the scene.**
*"Tugging out her eyelashes"* → then *"Sophie said it out loud without a moments hesitation."*
Read those together: she is falling apart and she doesn't hesitate. That's not a contradiction,
it's a character — scared and doing it anyway. Tier 3 asks for feelings through action instead
of labels, and you built one *out of the gap between* the action and the words. First draft, no
flag from me.

✅ **Focus skill (+5): "let them talk."** The whole entry is people speaking in their own words.
Zero reported speech. That's the fifth entry running — Style is ready for Tier 3.

🔧 **One thing, and it's rule-shaped, so you get the question first, not the fix.**
Here are your three tags, side by side:

> `"Ok", Sophie said.`
> `"I give you my word of the alliance". Sophie said it…`
> `"…before it's too late." Tarina replied hastly.`

**What do those three have in common?** (You've answered this question correctly on the first

🚀 **Stretch challenge (+10 XP, optional): give Sophie the last line — but not with words.**
Fitz just accused her of violating a treaty in front of a troll. The scene currently ends on

📌 Two small ones, fixed here and not made into anything: **treason → treaty** (a treason is a
