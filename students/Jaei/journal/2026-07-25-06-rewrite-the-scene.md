## Tutor feedback

🌟 **Two things you did well:**
1. *"watching the other team with a trembling hand, clutched to each other"* — real show-don't-tell. You didn't say "they were scared," you showed it in their hands and bodies. Exactly the Tier 3 move we're going for.
2. *"frantically looked around for the exit, but it was all sealed shut"* — this raises the stakes and makes the "no choice" ending feel earned instead of just stated.

🔧 **One thing to level up (verb check — Watch list):** *"Amy and Dan **was** watching the other team..."* When two people are joined by **and**, they're a plural subject, so it needs **were**: *"Amy and Dan **were** watching the other team..."* Same pattern as a couple entries back — reading it out loud before saving would catch it.

🚀 **Stretch challenge (+10 XP, optional):** The rival team's threat is paraphrased right now — *"they told them that they knew what they were doing and should hand them over the lead."* Since Tier 3 is about making the scene feel like a missing page from the real book, try turning that into an actual quoted line the rival team says out loud.
