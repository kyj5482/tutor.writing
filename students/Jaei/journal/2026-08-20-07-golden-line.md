🌟 **You zoomed in on a phrase instead of a word, and it was the better call.**
*"'I have to do it' gets primally used by Sophie Foster, but this time it's Tam who's using it."*
Messenger hands one character's signature line to a different character. You didn't say "good
line" — you said *whose* line it normally is. Naming who owns a phrase is what turns a quote into
proof.

🌟 **Your prediction comes out of the technique, not the plot.**
*"Which means it can indicate that next book might be all about Tam."* Most predictions guess from
events. Yours reads a craft choice and infers from it. That's a critic's prediction.
