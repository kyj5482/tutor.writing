## My writing

What would you do if you were in a place where you can either keep fighting or give up. Sophie Foster in Keeper of the Lost Cities by Shannon Messenger chose to fight against the Neverseen. An evil organiztaion trying to take over the Lost Cities.

The "big idea" behind this book is not just simply to enjoy the adventure Sophie and her friends endure, but to remind us that "we always have a choice even when we think we don't", and to make the right decision. One thing that I believe that Messenger did a great job on is the timing. It's incredible that she always have a good timing for us when to put in the action. It makes us as a reader to focus more and deeper in the book.

I would give this book a 4.5 star out of 5 because, even though her timing is really good, in this book the action happened at the end of the book. I, as a reader thinks that it should be somewhere in the middle where I can focus more.
