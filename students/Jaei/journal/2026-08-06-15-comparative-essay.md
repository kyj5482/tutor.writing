## My writing

Both Nightfall and Lodestar have a similar ending for an adventure they always go. The difference is that in Lodestar Messenger reveals the story slow, but in Nightfall, right from the start, it was a fast paced book, the slow one buys the Ahh moment, but the fast one gives more action.

In Lodestar, Messenger reveals the story slow, but in Nightfall, it was a start of action right from the start. For example, when Sophie says, "Let's go to the ravagog", in the middle part of the book, this clearly shows that it is a slow revealing book. This slow reveal gives an "ahh" moment. But. this really slow reveal costs her to make some small actions to prevent the readers from getting bored.

In the Nightfall, the action starts on the very first chapter. For example, "where are my parents?" in the first chapter proves it, because a question means the situation already happened. This shows that Nightfall's story goes in way faster pace than Lodestar because Nightfall's action starts from the beginning but, Lodestar's action starts in the middle of the book. Nightfall could start the action that fast because Lodestar was a big set-up for Nightfall.

Reading both books shows that this is about the whole series, and not about the one book. If Messenger focused differently on different book, it wouldn't be the series. So even though the Lodestar didn't have that much action, it was worth it because we are looking at the bigger picture, the whole series.
