## ⚠️ The diagnosis that matters — why the verb error won't die

He has been corrected on compound subjects **seven times in two months** and the streak is
still 0/3. That is not carelessness. Look at what he actually writes:

| What he wrote | The verb agrees with… |
|---|---|
| *"Teresa and Aris **was**"* (06-23) | Aris |
| *"me and my sister **was**"* (07-07) | my sister |
| *"trust and loyalty **is**"* (07-14) | loyalty |

| *"the plot and the action **is**"* (07-23) | the action |
| *"Amy and Dan **was**"* (07-25) | Dan |

**He is matching the verb to the nearest noun, not to the whole subject.** Every single

agreement") — and *nobody has ever explained the rule to him.* He has only ever been
handed the corrected sentence.

**So stop correcting it and teach it once.** → `lessons/compound-subjects.md`

nod and repeat the error next week. Six weeks of evidence says so.

## 🧯 2026-08-01 — a ban with no alternative gets routed around

> **A.** Find the exact line → quotation marks earned.

>

one real citation he'd deleted.** Item retired, 3/3.
